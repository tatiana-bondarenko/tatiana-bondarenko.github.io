I am the director of the [Working on Language in the Field (WOLF) Lab](https://fieldlinguistics.github.io/): a group of faculty members and students who conduct linguistic fieldwork on understudied and underdocumented languages across the world. Our work involves applying tools of formal linguistics to different languages, typological research, and language documentation and preservation work. Here are a couple of pictures of us: in the lab (2024), on an Alabama fieldtrip (2026), and on a Georgian fieldtrip (2025).
