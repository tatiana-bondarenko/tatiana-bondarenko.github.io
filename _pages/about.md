I am an Assistant Professor in [Linguistics at Harvard](https://linguistics.fas.harvard.edu/), and the director of the [WOLF-lab](/lab/). I study syntax and semantics, and I do fieldwork. The topics I am often thinking about are syntax and semantics of clausal embedding, argument and event structure, factivity/veridicality, verbal mood and polarity, cyclicity and locality of syntactic dependencies, complex agreement systems, and discontinuous noun phrases. A lot of my work is based on Altaic (Azeri, Balkar, Buryat, Korean) and South Caucasian (Georgian) languages; I have also done projects with Javanese and Russian. In 2024, my lab has started a collaboration with the [Alabama-Coushatta Tribe of Texas](https://www.alabama-coushatta.com/) on documentation and preservation of the Alabama language (Muskogean).

Before coming to Harvard, I got my PhD at [MIT](https://linguistics.mit.edu/) in 2022, and Master's and Bachelor's degrees from [Lomonosov Moscow State University](http://tipl.philol.msu.ru/index.php).

When I am not doing linguistics, I am usually working on gymnastic skills, dancing, or learning how to shoot 16mm film. I am a member [of the MIT gymnastics team](https://gymnastics.mit.edu/) and [Millenium Film Workshop](https://www.millenniumfilm.org/).
